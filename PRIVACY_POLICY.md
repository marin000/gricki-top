## 1. Who we are

Grički top (“**we**”, “**us**”, “**our**”) is a mobile application about Zagreb’s noon cannon at Lotrščak Tower. It offers a countdown to noon, a digital cannon experience, a timing challenge, daily stories, and quizzes.

## 2. Scope

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, and share information when you use the Grički top mobile application (“**App**”) and our related API services (“**Services**”).

---

## 3. Summary

- We do **not** require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, or phone number to use the App.
- We assign your device a **random identifier** and a secret token so we can sync your progress (stories read, quiz results, timing attempts, and settings) with our servers.
- Notifications about noon are **local reminders** scheduled on your device. We do **not** use a remote push notification service for this feature.
- We do **not** show advertisements in the App and do **not** sell your personal data.
- You can delete your server-side data from the App (or by contacting us).

---

## 4. Information we collect

### 4.1 Information you provide through use of the App

- **App settings:** sound enabled/disabled, haptics enabled/disabled, noon reminder preference, and reminder lead time
- **Story progress:** which stories you have read and related progress markers (for example, dismissing a story prompt)
- **Quiz activity:** quiz attempts, answers, and results associated with your device
- **Timing challenge results:** your tap timing attempts relative to noon (for example, offset from noon and related attempt metadata)

### 4.2 Information collected automatically

- **Device identifier:** A random ID generated on your device and stored locally in the device secure storage. It is sent to our servers to associate your synced data with your installation. It is **not** your hardware ID, advertising ID, or any identifier tied to your real-world identity.
- **Device token:** A random secret generated on your device, stored locally, and used (in hashed form on the server) to authenticate API requests for your installation.
- **Technical device metadata:** platform (iOS or Android), app version, locale, and last-seen timestamp, used to operate and maintain the Services.
- **Technical request data:** When you use our API, our hosting provider may process standard request metadata (such as IP address, request timestamps, and error logs) for security, reliability, and operation of the Services. We do not use this information to identify you personally.

### 4.3 Information stored only on your device

The following data stays on your device (or is primarily used on-device) and is not used for advertising:

- A local copy of your device identifier and token (in secure storage)
- Local caches of stories/settings and an offline outbox used to sync changes when connectivity returns
- Locally scheduled noon reminder notifications (if you enable them)

### 4.4 Device permissions

- **Notifications (optional):** If you grant permission, the App can schedule **local** reminders shortly before noon. These notifications are created on your device; we do not send remote push notifications for this feature.
- **Audio:** The App plays the cannon sound and related audio in the App. We do **not** record microphone audio for this experience.

We do **not** access your precise location, camera, photo library, contacts, SMS, or call logs. The App may open external links (for example maps or story source URLs) in your device browser or maps app when you choose to tap them; those third-party services have their own privacy policies.

## 5. How we use your information

We use the information described above to:

- Provide the noon countdown, cannon experience, timing challenge, stories, and quizzes
- Sync and restore your progress and settings across App sessions for the same installation
- Authenticate your device to our API
- Schedule optional local noon reminders on your device
- Operate, maintain, secure, and improve the Services
- Enforce fair use / rate limits where applicable

We do **not** use your data for advertising, marketing profiling, or selling to data brokers.
